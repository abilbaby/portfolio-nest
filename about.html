Biography

Profile Overview

I'm Abil K Baby, currently pursuing an Integrated Master of Computer Applications (MCA) at De Paul Institute of Science & Technology Angamaly, affiliated with Mahatma Gandhi University, Kerala. My professional focus centers on building responsive web user interfaces and designing relational databases that support them under the hood.

I strongly believe in practical experimentation. Developing full-stack systems like **ORDON** (Laravel matching database) and **Child Vaccination System** (ASP.NET scheduling workflow) teaches me how to model complex features, establish type safety constraints, and structure APIs logically.

During my academic coursework, I completed a formal frontend engineering training internship at NeST Digital Academy, Kochi, which refined my agile developer collaboration practices and component architectural patterns using Angular 18, TypeScript, and Git.
